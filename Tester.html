Info:
  • Mouse: place blocks, toggle buttons/levers, recolor wires.
  • Drag: hold mouse in grid to move the view.
  • Rotate: click blocks to rotate orientation.
  • Erase: click to erase cells.
  • Select: drag a rectangle to select an area.
  • Measure: click two cells to measure distance.
  • Inspect: click to see cell info in console.
  • Freeze: pause/resume simulation.
  • Auto-Route: click start and end to auto-wire.
  • Multi-Place: hold mouse to paint continuously.
